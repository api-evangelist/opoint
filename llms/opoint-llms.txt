# Opoint

> Opoint is a global news data platform that collects, structures, and delivers real-time online news from 250,000+ sources across 230 jurisdictions and 135 languages. Founded in 2002 and headquartered in Denmark, Opoint processes over 3.5 million articles per day and delivers them as structured, enriched data via REST API and JSON feeds.

## About Opoint

Opoint transforms raw online news into machine-readable, structured data. The platform is used by media monitoring companies, compliance teams, financial institutions, and business intelligence providers who need reliable, real-time access to global news content. Opoint is a data infrastructure provider, not an end-user tool, meaning its technology powers the products and services of other companies.

Key facts:
- 250,000+ curated global online news sources
- 3,500,000+ articles collected per day
- 230 jurisdictions covered
- 135 languages supported
- Sub-5-minute latency from publication to delivery
- 20+ years in operation (founded 2002)
- Offices in Denmark, Norway, United Kingdom, and India

### SafeFeed
A real-time, comprehensive feed of global news articles delivered in structured JSON format. SafeFeed provides the full firehose of Opoint's news data, filterable by language, country, region, and keywords. Clients can request addition of specific sources. SafeFeed processes approximately 4 million articles per day.

### StoredSearch
A filtered news feed based on saved search profiles. Users register Boolean search queries (supporting Boolean operators, frequency searches, and SPAN searches) and receive a continuous stream of matching articles in real time. Available via pull (API polling) or push (webhook delivery).

### Search API
A historical search API for querying Opoint's full article archive. Supports flexible filters including date range, source, language, and country. Designed for research, due diligence, and retrospective analysis. Pagination supports up to 500 articles per query.

### Tracking
An article-matching service that identifies when content similar to a given article appears across the web. Useful for tracking the spread of press releases, monitoring content republication, and measuring media reach. Uses a similarity scoring system (threshold: 20/100). Articles can be uploaded via API or FTP.

### Portal
A web-based news aggregation interface for real-time monitoring and exploration of global news data. Integrates with Opoint's data infrastructure and provides continuous updates.
